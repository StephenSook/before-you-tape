Before You Tape extension privacy

What the panel reads

The panel reads the item details you type or paste. After you agree to the notice, it reads the tab's title and link when you click the toolbar button. It does not read the page.

What stays on your laptop

Your item details stay in the open panel. Chrome keeps the clicked tab details for the browser session. It saves your choices and a copy of the rules on your laptop.

What leaves your laptop

After you agree to the notice, the panel downloads the public rules from our site. The request includes no item details, tab link or sign-in credentials.

Cloud checks are off in this version. If a later version offers a cloud check, it will ask first. That check sends the product details you paste, and the link if you add one, to Google's Gemini through our site.

Limited Use

We use your data only to provide the check you ask for. We do not sell the data, use it for ads or let people read your item details. We follow the Chrome Web Store's Limited Use rules.

How to remove your data

Click Clear in the panel or uninstall the extension. Clear also resets your choices.
