# Support

Before You Tape checks the items you plan to mail abroad with USPS, before you tape the box. Point
the camera at an item's label. The app reads it and shows one of three verdicts: OK to pack, Check
this, or Take it out. A model only reads the label. The rules decide, and each verdict shows the
rule behind it and the date that rule was checked.

## What it does

- Reads a label photo on your device when it has a built-in model, with no network needed.
- Checks the whole box for the country you pick, from the rule edition it carries.
- Asks you a question when the label alone cannot decide, and uses your answer.
- Sends a photo, or typed label words on iPhone, to the cloud only if you allow it. See
  [Privacy](/privacy/).

## What it does not do

- It does not mail anything, buy postage or fill in customs forms.
- It does not replace USPS or the destination's customs. When a rule may be out of date, the item
  reads "Check this" until the rule is read again.
- It cannot see inside a sealed package or read a label it cannot see.
- The rule words it looks for on a label are in English only.

## Common questions

**Why does an item say "Check this"?** The rules need something the label does not show, or a rule
it depends on is out of date. Open the item to see why and answer its question.

**Why does it ask about the cloud?** Your device could not read that item by itself. Nothing goes
to the cloud unless you tap Allow, and you can turn cloud checks off again.

**My iPhone does not read photos.** Photos are read on the iPhone on iOS 27 when Apple
Intelligence is on, its model has downloaded, and the iPhone's language is one it supports
(Settings > Apple Intelligence & Siri). Without it, you can allow cloud checks, or add the item by
hand with Type it in Your box.

**How do I delete my data?** See [Privacy](/privacy/).
