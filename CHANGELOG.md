- `src/capture/steady.ts`: the hold-zone steady detector and `sharpness`. - `src/engine/battery.ts` and `rules/label-words.json`: label words now count as battery evidence even when the model misses the battery flag or picks the wrong category. "Power bank", "portable charger" or "battery pack" on the label makes the battery loose ("Take it out"), except on a phone, tablet, laptop, smartwatch or earbuds, whose own sticker often says "battery pack". "Li-ion", "lithium ion", "lithium polymer", "Li-Po" and "rechargeable" count as battery evidence, and the lithium-ion words set the chemistry. Before, a power bank read as other electronics could reach "OK to pack", and so could an RC car labelled "LI-ION BATTERY INSIDE" with the flag missed. Source: second-model review (Fable), finding C2b. - `src/engine/edition.ts` and `src/engine/layers.ts`: dates must exist on the calendar (no month 13, no February 30), in the edition and in the date the caller passes as today.
